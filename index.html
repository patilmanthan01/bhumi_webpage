Problem Statement & Agricultural Solution

Empowering Precision Agriculture with Intelligent Decision Support

Challenge Faced by Farmers: Farmers face difficulties in selecting suitable crops and fertilizers due to variations in soil nutrients, rainfall, climate, and location.

The BHUMI Solution: The system provides location-specific crop, fertilizer, and crop-protection recommendations using structured agricultural data and Python-based rule logic.
